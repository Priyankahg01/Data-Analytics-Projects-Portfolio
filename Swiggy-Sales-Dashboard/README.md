# Swiggy Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive sales dashboard developed to analyze Swiggy food-order data and provide meaningful business insights. The project focuses on key sales, customer satisfaction, order, and performance metrics to support data-driven decision-making.
The raw dataset was sourced from Kaggle and contains information related to food orders, restaurants, locations, food categories, pricing, ratings, and customer reviews.

## Raw Data

The dataset contains the following columns:
- State
- City
- Order Date
- Restaurant Name
- Location
- Category
- Dish Name
- Price
- Rating
- Rating Count

The raw data was used as the foundation for analysing sales performance, customer ratings, order trends, food categories, and geographical sales distribution.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

1. Total Sales
Represents the overall revenue generated from food orders.

2. Average Rating
Represents the average customer satisfaction level across restaurants.

3. Average Order Value
Represents the average revenue generated per food order.

4. Ratings Count
Represents the total number of customer reviews.

5. Total Orders
Represents the total number of food orders received.

## Business Requirement Analysis

The dashboard was developed based on business requirements focused on understanding sales performance, customer satisfaction, order trends, food-type contribution, and geographical performance.

## Dashboard Charts and Analysis

1. Monthly Sales Trend
Shows how total sales fluctuate from month to month and helps identify changes and patterns in monthly sales performance.

2. Daily Sales Trend
Highlights variations in orders and revenue across different days of the week to understand daily sales patterns.

3. Total Sales by Food Type
Compares the revenue contribution of Veg and Non-Veg food categories and helps understand the contribution of different food types to overall sales.

4. Total Sales by State
Uses a map visualization to display the geographical distribution of revenue across different states and identify high-performing regions.

5. Quarterly Performance Summary
Provides a combined view of sales, ratings, and orders by quarter to evaluate overall business performance across different periods.

6. Top 5 Cities by Sales
Identifies the top five cities contributing the highest revenue and highlights the major cities driving sales performance.

7. Weekly Trend Analysis
Monitors weekly fluctuations in sales to identify consistency, changes, and recurring sales patterns.
